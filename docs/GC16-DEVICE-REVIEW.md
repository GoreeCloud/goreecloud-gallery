# gc.16 representative-device review target

The gc.16 acceptance target is the dialog-geometry issue observed on the gc.15 representative-device screenshots: sorting/grouping/filter dialog content should remain attached to its rounded Glaze surface without a large unused vertical region.

Acceptance criteria:

- dialog content measures to its controls by default;
- scrolling remains available when content exceeds the available viewport;
- the gc.15 rounded Glaze surface, semantic control accents, and 48dp targets remain intact;
- dialog title, controls, note text, and action buttons remain readable at normal and increased display/font sizes;
- sorting, grouping, filtering, confirmation, permissions, and file-operation behavior remain unchanged.

This is acceptance documentation, not proof of device acceptance. Actual device evidence remains required before Stable release.
